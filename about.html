ILIMIBRIDGE KEBBI is a proposed digital learning initiative designed to connect learners and educators with accessible learning opportunities.

Building a bridge between curiosity and opportunity.

We believe technology can make learning resources easier to discover and share. The project is being developed with learners and educators in Kebbi State in mind.

The long-term vision includes learning materials, practice quizzes, educator contributions and options that can support people with limited internet access.
